## **Veracode Static Scan Review & Rescan Process**

**Project: BTS Debit Card VRU**

---

### **Step 1: Access Veracode**

Navigate to the Veracode portal via the following link:
🔗 [https://sfsso.bnymellon.net/veracode](https://sfsso.bnymellon.net/veracode)

---

### **Step 2: Locate the Application**

From the Veracode dashboard, click on **“BTS Debit Card VRU”**.
📸 *Refer to Screenshot – Step 2*

---

### **Step 3: Find the Latest Static Scan**

You can locate the latest static scan in one of two ways:

* Under the **Application Profile** section.
* From the project labeled **“defendBot-dcv”**.

Choose the most recent scan, preferably aligned with the latest release cut.
📸 *Refer to Screenshot – Step 3*

---

### **Step 4: Select the Latest Release Cut**

From the list of available scans, select the scan corresponding to the **latest release cut** of **defendbot**.
📌 *In this example, it is the most recent post-release cut version.*
📸 *Refer to Screenshot – Step 4*

---

### **Step 5: Open and Review the Module**

Click **Open** to view the scan details.
In the module view interface, click on **“Review Modules”** to proceed.
📸 *Refer to Screenshot – Step 5*

---

### **Step 6: Modify Module & Rescan**

Scroll down the module list and **uncheck** the entry for:

> **spring-security-web.jar**

After deselecting this module, initiate a **Rescan** to update the scan results.
📸 *Refer to Screenshot – Step 6*
